Built on real research, not assumptions.

Before writing a single line of code, the team ran 136 employee surveys and 5 in-depth interviews across European companies. Every feature in Prokevo addresses a gap those employees described.

48% of employees say a daily streak feature would make them feel most successful at work.

Why current systems fail

The suggestion box is forgotten before Monday.

Companies invest in engagement tools but watch participation collapse after 3 months. Research confirms the three root causes are structural, not motivational.

No feedback

"Lack of feedback on submitted ideas" was the top friction employees reported — more damaging than repetitive tasks or slow approvals.

No visibility

Managers only see engagement problems after they surface in annual surveys. By then, the most motivated employees have already stopped trying.

No connection

Knowledge from one department never reaches another. Every team solves the same problems independently, wasting time and good ideas.
